## What this is

The v1.9 golden gate is retired **NO-GO**: two local no-egress golden executions measured zero
finding recall against 34 frozen `present` opportunities in the golden manifest
(`results/v1.9-golden-opportunity-manifest.json`), which are the identical 34 opportunities recorded
as `missed_defect_opportunity_ids` in `results/v1.9-golden-correction-ledger-v2.json` (verified
identical to the initial `v1.9-golden-correction-ledger.json`). Those 34 items are already-frozen,
already-trusted ground truth; nothing in this note or the JSON record relabels them.

ADR-0011 `required_before_a_future_cycle[0]` in `results/v1.9-golden-gate-no-go.json` asks whether a
future cycle should "evaluate whether the pack can support any cross-file/interprocedural tracing
... versus its current single-function pattern-matching architecture" before further per-miss
patching. Action item 3 operationalizes that question for the 34 already-known misses: for
engineering understanding only, classify each as **file-local** (plausibly resolvable within one
file, in reach of the detector's current single-file pattern-matching architecture) or
**cross-module** (plausibly requires resolving evidence across file/module boundaries, a capability
the detector does not currently have) under the LLM-PRV-001, LLM-EVL-001, and LLM-EVL-002 evidence
contracts. This is a retrospective, descriptive scoping exercise. It produces no new opportunity, no
new label, and no detector run, and it does not itself decide how large the cross-file tracing
investment should be — it sizes the population that investment would need to cover.

## Scope actually covered

Action item 3 defines an explicit local-scope contract for three rules, each already worded in
`docs/packs/free-llm-v1-rule-contract.md` to reject inference from evidence "elsewhere in the
repository":

- `LLM-PRV-001` — a local model invocation (directly or via an observably-called local helper) before
  the result emission it anchors on.
- `LLM-EVL-001` — case iteration, filtering, aggregate calculation, and emission forming one locally
  traceable chain.
- `LLM-EVL-002` — the producer and judge model/configuration both resolvable, with no inference of a
  distinct judge from elsewhere in the repository.

Those three rules cover 24 of the 34 present opportunities. The remaining 10 span `LLM-AGY-001` (2),
`LLM-VAL-001` (2), `LLM-IOH-001` (2), and `LLM-DAT-001` (4) — rules whose frozen contract already
recognizes a *resolved inter-procedural* path as in-scope at reduced confidence, unlike the three
named rules above. For those 10 rows the JSON record asks the same file-local/cross-module
architecture question using that rule's own already-frozen local/resolved-inter-procedural split, and
flags each such row (`contract_in_scope_of_action_item_3: false`) for owner confirmation that
extending the classification to these rules is the intended scope.
