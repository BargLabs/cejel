a model). A citation whose own recorded rationale explicitly admits uncertainty is, by that

## Limitations

- Second-component citations identify the specific file/module the required evidence chain needs;
  they do not themselves constitute a full trace of that second file's contents in every case. Round
  4 sharpened this into an operative rule rather than only a caveat: a citation resting on an
  imported module's name/context, without its content having been read to confirm the required
  element, is not sufficient for a confident classification and is now recorded ambiguous.
  `source_access_accounting` in the JSON record distinguishes files whose content was actually read
  from files only confirmed to exist by name or import.
- This document does not attempt to resolve whether the original opportunity's `present` ground
  truth is correct — only where, architecturally, the decisive evidence for the applicable rule
  physically lives, and only using evidence admissible under the strict per-row boundary and
  full-chain treatment described above.
- The 10 rows outside the three explicit action-item-3 contracts remain pending explicit owner scope
  acceptance regardless of how confidently their architecture question was answered here — and, as of
  round 4, none of the 10 currently resolve to confident cross-module at all (9 ambiguous, 1
  file-local).
- 26 of 34 rows are recorded ambiguous. This is an honest reporting of evidence gaps — most because
  the strict boundary excludes the only second-component evidence found, because only part of a
  multi-element required-evidence chain could be admissibly resolved, or because a citation rested on
  an imported module's name rather than its actually-read content — not a claim that these rows are
  actually file-local.
- Four full rounds of Codex reconciliation, each independently re-verifying the prior round's own
  output, have each found real defects in what the previous round had asserted as reconciled and
  correct — including, in round 4, a defect (import-name-only citations) that had been present since
  round 1 for some rows and was caught only on the fourth pass, by a reviewer whose own round's
  companion pass returned `PASS`. This is disclosed as a reason for reduced, not increased,
  confidence in the current split: a fifth review round could plausibly find further defects,
  including among the 7 rows still recorded confident.
