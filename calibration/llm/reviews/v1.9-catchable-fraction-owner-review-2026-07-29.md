  `AgentRuntime.kt` and `velune/models/probes.py` were not opened — their existence and role are
  established by the anchor file's own comment or import statement, per `source_access_accounting`).

  physically lives relative to the anchored file, and only using evidence admissible under the
  strict per-row boundary described above.

  acceptance regardless of how confidently their architecture question was answered here.
- 15 of 34 rows are recorded ambiguous. For most, this is because the strict boundary excludes the
  only second-component evidence found (a reverse caller, a same-package sibling, or a
  package-internal file) — not because no plausible second file exists, only because it cannot be

  This is an honest reporting of an evidence-boundary gap or of genuine reviewer disagreement, not a
  claim that these rows are actually file-local.
