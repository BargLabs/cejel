# Proposed ADRs

These records turn commercial hypotheses and governance decisions into falsifiable proposals.
Vertical-discovery proposals are maintained privately; this public series carries methodology and
governance proposals only.
**Proposed** means neither a shipped capability nor an approved customer claim. A proposal may advance
only by meeting its stated evidence gates; it may also be rejected without creating a product.
