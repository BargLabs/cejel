# Addendum 02 — no positive-result threshold

Status: preregistered before any run. This additive clarification supersedes only the
phrase “predeclared positive anchor” in Addendum 01; it changes no seed, criterion,
detector, rubric, `cited` predicate, held-out assignment, control, or aggregate guard.

The `20/30` calculation in Addendum 01 is an **illustration of the reporting rule**, not
a pass mark, publication threshold, decision boundary, or target outcome. There is no
qualitative distinction between 19/30 and 20/30.

For every non-void, non-refused held-out aggregate outcome `k` from 1 through 29, report
only `k/30`, its two-sided 95% Wilson interval, and the fixed-fixture limitation. The
licensed statement is: *on this fixed, isolated, in-scope held-out fixture set, Cejel
cited `k` of 30 named defect files; the 95% Wilson interval for that fixture-set
proportion is `[L, U]`.* No outcome-dependent threshold, label, or claim expansion is
permitted.

The existing `0/30` and `30/30` aggregate refusal, and the positive-control void rule,
remain in force. No scan, fixture materialization, evaluation, or recall calculation is
authorized by this addendum.
